I used to hide work until it looked finished. That trained me to overclaim. A README with a roadmap and an empty `src/` is still work. It is not a platform. The difference belongs in the first sentence, not in a footnote after a visitor has already decided you shipped it.

This site has three statuses on purpose: active, experimental, archived. Experimental means I will still change the design and I will not pretend there is a running control plane. Tracey is that today: reproduction-first incident resolution as a written gate, not as a service you can curl. Pullfrog Azure is the same kind of honesty with a different stack.

Archived is the other direction. Coursework stays public and labeled so I do not launder a school API into “systems I ran.” Active is the small set I will still maintain: this site, the DevEx plugin, the things with a check command and a deploy.

The portfolio trick I refuse is screenshot-driven status. A Figma of a dashboard is not experimental software. A repo with a design doc and a failed spike might be. If I cannot say what does not exist yet, the status is marketing.

Putting Tracey next to a live Vite site is slightly awkward. That is the point. Visitors can filter. I would rather be filterable than smooth.
